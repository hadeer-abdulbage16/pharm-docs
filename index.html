eStock System

eStock is a pharmacy management system designed to streamline daily operations such as inventory tracking, sales management, purchasing, and invoice printing.

Purpose

The main goal of eStock is to simplify pharmacy management by offering tools to control stock levels, monitor sales, and customize invoice printing.

Core Modules

  • Inventory Management
  • Sales & Invoicing
  • Purchases
  • Stores Management
  • Invoice Print Settings
  • Reports

Features

  • Multi-store support
  • Customizable invoice templates
  • Stock tracking and alerts
  • Flexible workflows
  • Configurable settings

Technology Stack

  • Backend: Laravel
  • Frontend: jQuery & Bootstrap
  • Database: MySQL
